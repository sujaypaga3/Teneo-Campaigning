What we lock in discovery

A short, focused discovery per build pins down the logic and the access each flow depends on. These are decisions, not development; once they're agreed, the build is configuration.

04 Share of Voice Reporting
  • Boolean query: exact Tesco and peer-brand search strings, plus the reporting window
  • Relevance & dedup rules: minimum relevance score and how duplicates are removed
  • Categorisation logic: the system prompt must replicate the rules Jonny & Lucy apply by hand today; this is the most important decision
  • Signal AI access: confirm API route, or the SharePoint folder for the CSV fallback
  • Template mapping: every figure mapped to its Excel named range and PowerPoint placeholder
  • Distribution: who reviews in Teams and how the client receives the final files
05 Social & Digital Channel Audit
  • Account list: client and competitor handles for LinkedIn, X and Instagram, owned by Evan in SharePoint
  • Platform access: LinkedIn API tier, X paid tier, and Instagram's 2–4 week Meta app review (or the CSV path per platform)
  • Metrics set: the followers, engagement and top-post fields captured per platform
  • Analysis framework: theme categories, benchmarking method, C-suite criteria and output format
  • Report structure: sections, benchmarking table and recommendations layout
  • Delivery: confirmed as the Copilot Studio chat in Teams, with follow-up questions supported
